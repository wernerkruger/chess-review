How classification works

!! Brilliant – a sound piece sacrifice that is (nearly) the best move, from a position that was not already won.
! Great – the only good move, or the move that punishes an opponent's mistake and swings the game.
★ Best – the engine's top choice (0.00 expected points lost).
✓ Excellent – loses ≤ 0.02 expected points.
✓ Good – loses 0.02 – 0.05.
📖 Book – a known opening move (Lichess ECO database).
→ Forced – the only legal move.
?! Inaccuracy – loses 0.05 – 0.10.
? Mistake – loses 0.10 – 0.20.
✗ Miss – the opponent blundered into a lost position and the winning continuation was not found.
?? Blunder – loses more than 0.20 expected points.

Expected points = win probability derived from the Stockfish centipawn score (Win% = 50 + 50·(2/(1+e−0.00368·cp) − 1)). Move accuracy uses the Lichess curve 103.1668·e−0.04354·ΔWin% − 3.1669; game accuracy is the mean of the volatility-weighted and harmonic means of move accuracies (book and forced moves excluded). Thresholds follow chess.com's published ClassificationV2 table.
